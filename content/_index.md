# About Me

<br>

I am an Associate Professor at [EPITA](https://www.epita.fr/), and I conduct my
research at [EPITA Research Laboratory](https://www.lre.epita.fr) (LRE) within
the TIRF group. My research interests mainly focus on Mathematical Morphology,
and particularly on hierarchical representations of images. To this aim, I
maintain [Pylene](https://gitlab.lre.epita.fr/olena/pylene), a generic and
efficient image processing library, and its associated Python bindings,
[Pylena](https://gitlab.lre.epita.fr/olena/pylena).

My teaching activities focus on programming (*C++ programming, Advanced C++
programming, Python for Big Data*) and image processing, particularly within the
EPITA IMAGE major (but not restricted to). Since February 2025, I am the co-head
of the EPITA IMAGE major with [Élodie
Puybareau](https://www.lre.epita.fr/perso/%C3%A9lodie-puybareau).


I obtained an Engineering Degree in Computer Science at EPITA in 2020, in the
IMAGE major doubled with the Research and Development major. I then obtained a
PhD from Sorbonne Université in 2023 on the subject of [A Generic, Efficient,
and Interactive Approach to Image Processing with Applications in Mathematical
Morphology](https://theses.hal.science/tel-04531954v1) under the supervision of
[Guillaume Tochon](https://www.lre.epita.fr/perso/guillaume-tochon), [Edwin
Carlinet](https://www.lre.epita.fr/perso/edwin-carlinet), and [Didier
Verna](https://www.lre.epita.fr/perso/didier-verna). After my thesis, from
November 2023 to August 2025, I worked at [Spimed-AI](https://www.spimed-ai.com)
on automatic Coronary Artery Dicease (CAD) diagnosis from Coronary CT
Angiography (CCTA).
