About Me

I am an Associate Professor at EPITA, and I conduct my research at EPITA Research Laboratory (LRE) within the TIRF group. My research interests mainly focus on Mathematical Morphology, and particularly on hierarchical representations of images. To this aim, I maintain Pylene, a generic and efficient image processing library, and its associated Python bindings, Pylena. More information is available here about my research activities.

My teaching activities focus on programming ( C++ programming, Advanced C++ programming, Python for Big Data ) and image processing, particularly within the EPITA IMAGE major (but not restricted to). Since February 2025, I am the co-head of the EPITA IMAGE major with Élodie Puybareau. More information is available here about my teaching activities.

I obtained an Engineering Degree in Computer Science at EPITA in 2020, with a major in Image Processing doubled with the Research and Development major. I then obtained a PhD from Sorbonne Université in 2023 on the subject of A Generic, Efficient, and Interactive Approach to Image Processing with Applications in Mathematical Morphology under the supervision of Guillaume Tochon, Edwin Carlinet, and Didier Verna. After my thesis, I worked at Spimed-AI on automatic Coronary Artery Dicease (CAD) diagnosis from Coronary CT Angiography until September 2025.
